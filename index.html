A CLOSER LOOK

Creative work grows through observation

Creative practice often begins before the first draft or sketch. Collecting references, noticing patterns, comparing approaches, and asking why a particular choice works all help build a vocabulary for making. Observation turns the surrounding world into material for thought and gives creative decisions a clearer foundation.

To explore this subject further, compare the examples in the topic guide above and notice how context changes the way each idea is understood.
