SITE INFORMATION

Privacy Policy.

How Lanternline Review handles information when you browse this website or prepare a message for the editorial team.

This policy describes the information practices of the static website as supplied in this package.

Information entered into the contact form

The contact form collects the name, email address, subject, and message that you enter in your browser. When you submit it, the website prepares an email draft in your device's email application. The message is sent only if you choose to send it from that application.

Website usage information

The website does not include an account system, analytics platform, advertising pixel, or embedded chat tool. The supplied pages do not store form details in a website database.

Cookies and browser storage

The supplied website does not set first-party cookies or use browser storage for tracking. No cookie preference banner is included because no non-essential cookies are used by the static pages as delivered.

External content delivery

Bootstrap files are loaded from the jsDelivr content delivery network. When your browser requests those files, the delivery provider may receive standard technical request information such as an IP address, browser details, and the requested file.

Messages and email providers

If you send an email draft, your email application and email provider handle the message according to their own privacy practices. Avoid including information that is not needed for an editorial question or correction.

External websites

If a future article links to another website, that website will have its own information practices and privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated if the website's features or information practices change. The current version will remain available from the site navigation and footer.
